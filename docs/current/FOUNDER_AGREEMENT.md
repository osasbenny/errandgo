## 2. Purpose
The founders intend to create, own and operate a technology marketplace currently using the working name `ErrandGo`, connecting customers with verified Runners for approved errands and related local services, initially through a controlled Lagos pilot.

## 3. Founder Relationship
The parties acknowledge that this is a **co-founder relationship**, not a client/vendor software-development engagement, unless otherwise amended in writing.

The Technical Founder is not providing the software build as an unpaid family favour or ordinary free service. The technical work is being contributed as founder sweat equity in exchange for the agreed ownership and continuing founder rights.

Likewise, the Product/Design/Operations Founder’s product, design, research and operational work is contributed as founder sweat equity.

## 4. Ownership / Cap Table
The final ownership split must be agreed before production engineering begins.

- Other: ______ %

**Total: 100%.**

No founder should assume that equal cash contributions automatically require equal equity, or that paying a larger startup expense automatically changes equity. Any equity change must be documented and signed.

## 5. Founder Contributions
The detailed contribution schedule in `FOUNDER_CONTRIBUTIONS.md` is incorporated by reference into this agreement.

### Founder A — Product / Design / Operations
Expected contribution includes product vision, customer/Runner research, UI/UX, design system, product management, pilot operations, Runner/customer acquisition, partnerships, marketing/growth support and other agreed non-technical responsibilities.

### Founder B — Technical Founder / CTO
Expected contribution includes technical architecture, frontend/mobile engineering, backend engineering, database/data model, admin dashboard, authentication/authorization, payments, KYC integration, maps/location, security, infrastructure, CI/CD, deployment, monitoring, technical documentation and continuing technical leadership.

The Technical Founder’s initial engineering contribution has an indicative commercial replacement value of approximately **₦10,000,000–₦15,000,000+**, depending on final scope. This valuation is for contribution/negotiation context only and does **not** create a ₦10m–₦15m debt owed by the company unless separately agreed in writing.

The Product/Design/Operations Founder’s contribution should also be documented with an agreed indicative value where useful.

## 8. Vesting
Proposed founder vesting:
- Vesting term: **48 months**.
- Cliff: **12 months**.
- Vesting after cliff: monthly, unless counsel recommends another structure.

Final legal implementation must be reviewed by counsel and coordinated with the company’s share structure.

Unvested equity should be subject to agreed repurchase/forfeiture treatment if a founder leaves or ceases substantial contribution before fully vesting.

## 9. Roles and Decision Rights
### Founder A primary authority
- Product/design execution.
- Customer/Runner research.
- Approved pilot operations.
- Marketing/growth execution within approved budget.
- Partnerships within agreed authority.

### Founder B primary authority
- Technical architecture.
- Engineering implementation.
- Infrastructure and security.
- Technical provider integration.
- Deployment/release process.
- Engineering standards and production reliability.

### Reserved matters requiring both founders
- New share issuance or changes to founder ownership.
- External investment/fundraising terms.
- Material borrowing/debt.
- Sale of the company or substantial assets/IP.
- Admission/removal of a founder.
- Material change in core business model.
- Unbudgeted spending above the agreed threshold.
- Related-party transactions.
- Major legal settlements.
- Dissolution/winding up.

## 10. Intellectual Property
All code, designs, brand assets, documentation, databases/data models, product specifications, internal processes and other work created specifically for the company should be assigned to the company through appropriate legal instruments.

Pre-existing IP owned by a founder must be identified in writing before it is incorporated into the product. Unless assigned, pre-existing IP remains with its original owner and any company licence must be documented.

Source repositories, cloud resources, domains and production provider accounts should ultimately be controlled by the company, with appropriate founder/admin access rather than remaining solely under one founder’s personal account.

## 11. Founder Compensation
During pre-revenue/pilot stage:
- Founder salaries: `TBD` / normally deferred until approved milestone.
- Reimbursable company expenses: permitted when approved and documented.

Suggested salary trigger for discussion: external funding, sustained operating revenue, or another jointly approved financial milestone.

Equity ownership and salary are separate. Holding founder shares does not mean a founder must work without salary forever once the company can reasonably compensate active founders.

## 12. Additional Funding
If additional capital is required:
1. The founders review the approved budget and reason for the cash call.
2. Each founder may contribute according to an agreed formula.
3. Unequal additional funding should normally be documented as a founder loan/reimbursable expense unless both founders explicitly agree that it changes equity.
4. External funding and dilution require reserved-matter approval.

## 13. Confidentiality and Security
Founders must protect confidential technical, financial, customer, Runner, operational and strategic information. Production credentials must not be shared casually or stored in public repositories/messages.

## 14. Conflicts of Interest
Founders must disclose material conflicts, related-party arrangements and competing interests, and must not divert company opportunities for personal benefit.

## 15. Founder Performance / Continuing Contribution
Both founders are expected to perform the responsibilities set out in `FOUNDER_CONTRIBUTIONS.md` and the current operating plan.

Persistent failure to contribute materially should trigger a documented founder review before any termination, role reduction, equity treatment or buyback action.

## 16. Founder Departure
The final legal agreement must specify:
- Treatment of vested and unvested shares.
- Good-leaver / bad-leaver rules where legally appropriate.
- Repurchase/buyback rights.
- Return of devices, data, credentials and company property.
- Transfer of repositories/provider accounts.
- Confidentiality obligations after departure.
- Treatment of ongoing customer, Runner, employee and partner relationships.

## 17. Death or Incapacity
Counsel should specify treatment of unvested shares, voting rights, succession/estate issues and operational continuity.

## 18. Deadlock
Because a closely held founder company can become unable to act during serious disagreement, the final agreement should define a deadlock process.

Proposed sequence:
1. Good-faith founder meeting with written issue statement.
2. Cooling-off period and second meeting.
3. Independent mediation.
4. Agreed buy-sell or other deadlock mechanism drafted by counsel.
5. Arbitration/court only as legally appropriate.
