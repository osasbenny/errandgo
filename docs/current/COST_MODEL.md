# ErrandGo Cost Model
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Pre-Engineering Budget  
**Date:** 9 October 2026

## 1. Purpose
This document separates founder sweat equity from actual cash required to activate third-party services, infrastructure and pilot operations.

The founders currently propose an initial startup cash budget of **₦500,000**.

## 2. Recommended Funding Structure
### Full startup funding available at kickoff
**Recommended project account balance before engineering setup begins: ₦500,000.**

This is company/project funding, not the Technical Founder’s software-development fee.

If the Product/Operations Founder is funding the full startup amount, record the ₦500,000 as an agreed capital contribution or founder loan. It does not automatically change equity.

### Minimum engineering activation release
If the founders prefer staged funding, the **minimum amount to make available immediately for proper engineering/provider setup is ₦300,000**, with the remaining **₦200,000 retained as project/pilot reserve**.

## 5. Costs Not Included in the ₦500,000 Unless Explicitly Approved
- Legal drafting/review of founder/shareholder documents.
- CAC incorporation/restructuring/share filings.
- Apple Developer Program if iOS launch is deferred.
- Paid advertising/content-creator campaign.
- Salaries/stipends.
- Large customer promotions/subsidies.
- Insurance.
- Background checks beyond normal provider/KYC charges.
- New hardware/test devices.
- Major third-party SaaS upgrades.

These should be separately approved rather than silently consuming engineering infrastructure funds.

## 6. Founder Sweat Equity vs Cash
### Technical Founder
Contributes engineering work as founder sweat equity. Indicative commercial replacement value: **₦10m–₦15m+**, subject to final scope.

### Product/Design/Operations Founder
Contributes product/design/operations work as founder sweat equity. Indicative value: founder-agreed amount.

### Cash
Cash used for Firebase, KYC, maps, domains and other external services is a **company expense**, not automatically the personal responsibility of the Technical Founder because those services are technical.

## 7. Monthly / Variable Cost Categories After Launch
Track separately:
- Firebase/Google Cloud usage.
- KYC checks.
- Maps/geocoding/routes.
- SMS/WhatsApp/email.
- Payment processing.
- Bank-transfer/payout fees.
- Storage/media.
- Monitoring/logging.
- Support/refunds/fraud losses.
- Marketing/customer acquisition.

## 8. Per-Errand Unit Economics Template
For each completed errand:

`Customer Service/Platform Revenue`

minus

`Payment Processing + Runner Payout Cost + KYC Allocation + Maps/Messaging Allocation + Promo Subsidy + Refund/Fraud Allocation + Support Allocation`

=

`Contribution Margin`

Founders should not treat gross transaction value as company revenue.

## 9. Cash Approval Rules
Recommended interim rule:
- Budgeted ordinary expense up to **₦50,000**: responsible founder may approve, with receipt/record.
- Single unbudgeted expense above **₦50,000**: both founders approve.
- Equity, loans, major commitments or recurring contracts: both founders approve regardless of amount.

The final legal/accounting policy should be reflected in the Founder Agreement.

## 10. Engineering Kickoff Funding Recommendation
**Preferred:** fund the full **₦500,000** project budget before technical kickoff so engineering does not stall for repeated small top-ups.

**Absolute practical minimum to begin proper backend/provider engineering:** **₦300,000** immediately available.

The remaining **₦200,000** should stay untouched as project/pilot reserve unless both founders approve another use.
