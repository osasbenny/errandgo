# ErrandGo Founder Contributions
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Founder Review  
**Date:** 9 October 2026

This document records what each founder is expected to contribute. It should be read with the Founder Agreement and reviewed whenever responsibilities materially change.

## 1. Founder A — Product / Design / Operations Founder
### Initial contribution
- Original product concept and problem framing.
- Product research and competitive research.
- Customer and Runner journey definition.
- UI/UX design for Customer, Runner and Admin experiences.
- Design system, visual language and interaction specifications.
- Product requirements input and product prioritization.
- Brand direction and naming collaboration.
- Lagos pilot operational planning.

### Ongoing contribution
- Product ownership/product management.
- User research and usability testing.
- Runner recruitment/onboarding operations.
- Customer acquisition and early growth activities.
- Partnerships and local-market operations.
- Support/dispute operational input.
- KPI review and product iteration.
- Marketing/content coordination as agreed.

### Indicative initial commercial replacement value
Founder-agreed amount: **₦____________**

If the founders currently estimate this at approximately **₦1,000,000**, record that as an indicative starting valuation only; it is not by itself a formula for determining equity.

## 2. Founder B — Technical Founder / CTO
### Initial contribution
- Product technical feasibility review.
- Technical architecture and system design.
- Repository and development-environment setup.
- Mobile/web frontend implementation.
- Backend/API/business-logic implementation.
- Authentication and role-based authorization.
- Database/data model and security rules.
- Admin Command Center.
- Payment integration and internal ledger logic.
- Runner KYC/identity-provider integration.
- Maps/geolocation functionality.
- Messaging/notifications integration.
- Storage and media handling.
- Security controls and audit logging.
- CI/CD, staging and production deployment.
- Monitoring, logging and error handling.
- QA support and production-readiness remediation.
- Technical documentation and handover records.

### Ongoing contribution
- Technical leadership.
- Production reliability and incident response.
- Security and dependency maintenance.
- Provider/API maintenance.
- Architecture evolution and scaling.
- Engineering roadmap and future hiring oversight.

### Indicative initial commercial replacement value
**₦10,000,000–₦15,000,000+**, subject to final MVP scope.

This is an indicative valuation of contributed professional capability, not an invoice to the company unless the founders separately create a paid development agreement.
