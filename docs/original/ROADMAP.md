# ErrandGo Product Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation (Weeks 1–2)
Finalize PRD, UX flows, database schema, architecture, legal/compliance review, provider accounts and pilot metrics.

## Phase 1 — MVP (Weeks 3–8)
Authentication; profiles; Runner verification; errand creation; discovery; acceptance; status tracking; chat; notifications; payment integration; ratings; admin dashboard.

## Phase 2 — Lagos Pilot (Weeks 9–12)
Launch in selected Lagos zones. Recruit and verify initial Runners. Run controlled customer acquisition. Monitor service quality, fraud, payments and support.

## Phase 3 — Optimization (Months 4–6)
Improve matching, pricing, retention, referrals, Runner supply, support automation and analytics.

## Phase 4 — Expansion (Months 6–12)
Expand to additional Lagos zones and then other Nigerian cities based on unit economics and operational readiness.

## Future
Business accounts; recurring errands; scheduled errands; corporate logistics; subscriptions; route optimization; automated pricing; partnerships with stores and restaurants.
