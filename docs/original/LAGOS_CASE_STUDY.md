# ErrandGo Lagos Market Case Study
**Status:** Planning hypothesis, not a claim of completed market research.

## Why Lagos
Lagos has a large, dense urban economy with frequent mobility, shopping, delivery and administrative needs. This makes it a logical test market for a local errand marketplace.

## Example User Problem
A busy professional in Lekki needs a document collected from Ikeja and delivered before close of business. Instead of relying on an unknown contact, the customer posts the task, sees a price, gets matched with a verified Runner, tracks progress and receives confirmation.

## Example Runner Problem
A Runner already operating around Lekki wants additional paid jobs without finding customers individually. The marketplace supplies nearby opportunities and records completed jobs and ratings.

## Pilot Hypothesis
Customers value convenience, visibility and trust enough to pay a transparent service price. Runners value consistent job supply enough to complete verification and accept marketplace commissions.

## Suggested Lagos Launch Pattern
Phase 1: one or two connected zones.
Phase 2: expand after reliable fulfillment.
Phase 3: add additional zones when Runner density and support capacity are sufficient.

## Competitive Position
Do not compete only on being cheaper. Compete on:
- Verified Runners
- Transparent prices
- Status tracking
- Customer support
- Ratings
- Reliable payout
- Simple user experience

## Risks
Traffic and travel time; inaccurate addresses; fraud; cancellations; item disputes; payment reversals; low Runner density; regulatory obligations.

## Research Required Before Investment
Conduct customer interviews, Runner interviews, competitor mapping, zone-level pricing research, CAC experiments and legal/compliance review. Replace this hypothesis document with measured pilot data after launch.
