# ErrandGo Business Model

## Core Revenue
Initial platform commission: 10% of the errand service amount, subject to review.

Example: ₦10,000 errand → ₦1,000 gross platform commission before payment fees, refunds, promotions and taxes.

## Additional Revenue Opportunities
- Featured Runner placement.
- Business accounts.
- Corporate errand plans.
- Scheduled/priority service fees.
- Customer convenience fees where transparent and legally appropriate.
- Partnerships and referral commissions.

## Unit Economics
Track:
Gross transaction value (GTV)
- Runner payout
- Payment/provider fees
- Refunds/chargebacks
- Promotions
- Support/fraud costs
= contribution margin.

## Marketplace Health
Key metrics: fill rate, time to acceptance, completion rate, repeat rate, average order value, Runner utilization, acquisition cost, customer lifetime value, dispute rate and contribution margin per completed errand.

## Pricing Principles
Keep pricing transparent. Customer sees total price before payment. Runner sees expected earnings. Any platform fee must be disclosed before checkout.

## Business Risks
Low Runner supply, fraud, failed deliveries, chargebacks, regulatory requirements, customer acquisition cost and low repeat usage.
