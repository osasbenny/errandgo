# ErrandGo Lagos Pilot Plan

## Objective
Validate that Lagos customers will repeatedly pay for convenient, trusted errands and that Runners can complete jobs profitably.

## Pilot Scope
Start with selected dense, serviceable Lagos zones rather than the entire state. Prioritize areas where customers, Runners and support can be reached reliably.

## Pilot Users
Target approximately 50–100 verified Runners and 100–250 early customers for the first controlled cohort.

## Initial Categories
Pickup & Delivery, Shopping, Document Collection, Food Pickup and Grocery Shopping.

## Operating Rules
- Manual approval of every Runner during pilot.
- Human support during operating hours.
- Conservative job radius.
- Clear cancellation rules.
- Manual review of unusual payments/payouts.
- No high-risk or prohibited items.
- Daily reconciliation.

## Pilot KPIs
Target hypotheses rather than fixed promises:
- ≥70% of posted eligible errands matched.
- ≥90% of accepted errands completed.
- Median acceptance time under 15 minutes.
- Customer rating ≥4.5/5.
- Dispute rate below 3%.
- Meaningful repeat usage by the end of the cohort.
- Positive contribution margin on mature job categories.

## Experiment Design
Run two or more customer acquisition channels. Test pricing/commission carefully. Compare Runner incentives. Interview customers and Runners weekly.

## Exit Criteria
Proceed to wider Lagos launch if service quality, repeat rate, safety and unit economics meet agreed thresholds. Otherwise narrow categories, adjust pricing or redesign matching.
